## GENOS

**Bioinformatics for everyone.** GENOS is a bioinformatics company based in **Puerto Rico**, helping scientific teams design and run computational biology and data analysis work—from multi‑omics pipelines and genomic analysis to contract-ready documentation and partner-facing resources.

---

### What we do

- Deliver **bioinformatics and computational biology** services across genomics, transcriptomics, and related analysis workflows, with an emphasis on reproducible methods and clear deliverables.
- Support **contracting and collaboration** for research institutions, startups, and partners who need structured scopes, reporting, and traceability for biodata projects.
- Build and curate **documentation, skills, and open materials** so teams can adopt best practices faster—aligned with how modern labs actually work.
