## 🧬 About GENOS

GENOS LLC focuses on **bioinformatics and computational biology** for research teams and industry partners: reproducible analysis workflows, clear deliverables, and collaboration across experiments, pipelines, and reporting.

---

## 🧪 What we do

- ✅ Deliver **bioinformatics and computational biology** services across genomics, transcriptomics, and related analysis workflows, with an emphasis on reproducible methods and clear deliverables.
- ✅ Support **contracting and collaboration** for research institutions, startups, and partners who need structured scopes, reporting, and traceability for biodata projects.
- ✅ Build and curate **documentation, skills, and open materials** so teams can adopt best practices faster—aligned with how modern labs actually work.

---

## 🔬 What we work on

We operate across the **tools**, **visuals**, and **scientific domains** typical of modern computational biology—without detailing internal systems or vendor stacks.

- **Tools** — Industry-standard bioinformatics software, pipelines, and reproducible workflows suited to collaborative delivery.
- **Graphics & presentations** — Figures, diagrams, and presentation-ready imagery suited to bioinformatics and life-science audiences (pathways, omics concepts, molecular storytelling).
- **Multi-omics** — Integrated analysis perspectives spanning genomics, transcriptomics, and related layers where projects require aligned interpretation.
- **Proteins** — Sequence-to-function framing, structural context, and protein-centric analyses where programs call for it.
- **3D modeling** — Structural and molecular visualization workflows for presentations and interpretation (e.g., structures, surfaces, binding context) when projects benefit from a three-dimensional view.
